Our Story

A Serious
Baseball Club
With an Udderly
Perfect Name

Built to win.
Born to tug.

The Udder Tuggers were founded in 2024 by a group of players already competing in the Pine Tree Men's Baseball League who were hellbent on building a new powerhouse. The idea was simple: bring together some of the best baseball talent from the Portland and Topsham area and see what we could build.

Many of the guys had played with or against each other in other leagues, and little by little, the pieces started coming together. The result was a team built to compete from day one.

The Tuggers reached the league championship in their inaugural season, returned in 2025 and captured the club's first title, then made the finals again in 2026. With each season, the roster has gotten stronger, the expectations higher, and the haters louder. Win or lose, the goal stays the same: keep getting better and keep chasing championships.
